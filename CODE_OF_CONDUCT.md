# Community conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Treat other contributors with patience and respect. Discuss ideas and code rather
than attacking people. Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds,
communication styles, and accessibility needs. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption
are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

When reporting a conduct concern, share the relevant context privately with the
maintainer once a private contact method is listed in the published repository.
Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.
